My Journey

The Path So Far

How I transitioned from academic research into building comprehensive digital ecosystems across code, vector, and video.

The Foundation

Understanding the Audience

My journey into media didn't start with code; it started with understanding how people respond to messaging. As a bachelor student, I completed an intensive final year research project analyzing the advertising effectiveness of local radio stations. This deep dive into marketing constraints, audience targeting, and campaign methodologies taught me a core truth: every design and every line of code must ultimately serve a human need.

Visual Identity

Mastering the Visual Vocabulary

I expanded my creative toolkit by stepping into the world of brand architecture and video production. Working with clients allowed me to translate ideas into tangible assets. For instance, I completely shaped the visual identity for Angee's Snacks, building everything from custom vector logos and packaging mockups to comprehensive wholesale product brochures.

Simultaneously, while working with client Little Heart Pet Shop, I took charge of their visual media—filming grooming processes, editing social reels in VN, and designing promotional banners to drive local engagement.

The Engineering Leap

Connecting Logic to Design

I realized that visual design was only half the battle; I wanted to build the engines that powered these digital experiences. I dove into full-stack development, mastering Python, Django, MySQL, and Bootstrap.

This culminated in engineering a robust, live grooming booking platform. Prioritizing cross-device accessibility, I ensured the platform was fully responsive across PCs, laptops, iPads, and mobile devices. It successfully processed over 300+ live bookings without breaking a sweat, proving my capability to merge beautiful frontend UI with secure backend architecture.

Present Day

Creative Designer at Swopu Creation

Today, I operate as a Creative Designer at Swopu Creation. My day-to-day involves working with international brands, producing motion graphics, and editing high-retention UGC videos. I also maintain an active freelance presence, recently drafting comprehensive service proposals and pitching structured design tiers directly to corporate executives. My goal remains the same: building systems that work and designing visuals that captivate.
